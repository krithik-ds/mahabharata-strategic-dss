Sanskrit Words & Simple Meanings Guide

Easy-to-understand explanations for ancient words used in the Mahabharata

Parva (पर्व) Book / Major Chapter

The Mahabharata has 18 major books (Parvas), like Adi Parva, Sabha Parva, Bhishma Parva, etc.

Adhyaya (अध्याय) Sub-chapter / Section

A specific episode or section inside a Parva.

Dharma (धर्म) Righteous Duty / Ethics

The moral law, ethical obligation, and righteous action that keeps society and leadership balanced.

Rajadharma (राजधर्म) Duty of Leaders / Kings

The rules and principles of good corporate governance, fair leadership, and protecting stakeholders.

Agnatavasa (अज्ञातवास) Incognito / Hidden Exile

Living completely disguised without letting anyone know your true identity to avoid competitor sabotage.

Jatugriha (जतुगृह) House of Lac / Trap

A palace made of highly flammable materials built by Duryodhana to trap the Pandavas.

Dyuta (द्यूत) Rigged Dice Game

The unfair dice gambling game where Shakuni used magical loaded dice to cheat Yudhishthira.

Chakravyuha (चक्रव्यूह) Circular Maze Formation

A complex 7-tier circular defense trap in battle that is easy to enter but nearly impossible to exit without special knowledge.

Pashupatastra (पाशुपतास्त्र) Supreme Capability / Weapon

An invincible weapon acquired by Arjuna through intense discipline, symbolizing building an unbeatable tech or skills moat.

Swayamvara (स्वयंवर) Open Talent Contest

A public competition where kings showcased superior capability (archery) to win an alliance with Panchala.
